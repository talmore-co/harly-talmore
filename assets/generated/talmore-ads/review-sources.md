## Interpretation boundaries

Supporting methodology reading: [Kaiser et al., NIM Marketing Intelligence Review 18(1), 2026](https://www.nim.org/en/publications/detail/leaving-insight-to-digital-twins), "Leaving Insight to Digital Twins? Promise, Progress and Limits of Synthetic Respondents," DOI 10.2478/nimmir-2026-0008. Public article directly read. Finds synthetic respondents can reproduce broad patterns while overstating positive attitudes and understating variation. This supports using the exercise for early-stage concept screening, not estimating Talmore's response rates. Its study-specific numerical findings are not transferred to these ads.

- Six personas and two reviewer roles are synthetic AI outputs, not interviews or independent human experts.
- Creative preferences are hypotheses. No response rates, conversion probabilities, p-values or sample confidence intervals are supportable here.
- Simulated 3-second impressions describe a review lens, not a timed human experiment.
- Rank suitable completed applications and downstream recruiting quality over raw clicks. Threshold/event configuration must be verified before using QualifiedApplication for live optimization.
- Employment targeting settings and currently available account options should be checked at campaign setup; this creative review does not assert country-specific platform restrictions.
